# Answer key: demo lease flags (private, never served by the API)

Section numbers refer to `demo-lease.pdf`. Each item is "worth confirming with the landlord", not a legal conclusion.

| # | Section | Clause (short) | Why it is worth checking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9.1 | Tenant accepts the home "as is" and waives any move-in inspection or condition report | Without a condition report it is harder to show later what was already damaged. MoveMate's own report covers this, but ask whether the landlord will do a joint inspection. |
| 2 | 9.2 | Tenant pays for "all damage and wear", regardless of cause | Normal wear and tear and damage that existed before moving in are not defined. Ask what is excluded. |
| 3 | 5.1, 5.2 | Deposit of two months' rent; return "within a reasonable time"; deductions the landlord "considers justified" | No return date and no list of allowed deductions. Ask for a deadline and what can be deducted. |
| 4 | 8.3 | Flat 150 € cleaning fee at move-out, deductible from the deposit | A fixed fee applies even if the home is left clean. Ask whether it is avoidable. |
| 5 | 10 | Entry with 24 h notice "where possible", and without notice when the landlord "considers it necessary" | No clear limit on when entry without notice is allowed. Ask for a notice rule and what counts as an emergency. |
| 6 | 3.2, 11.1 | Automatic 12-month renewal unless 3 months' notice; early exit fee of two months' rent | Easy to miss the notice date, and leaving early is costly. Ask for the exact notice date and whether the fee can be reduced. |
| 7 | 4.2 | Rent can rise on 30 days' notice, no cap or frequency stated | Ask how often and by how much rent may change. |
| 8 | 7 | Home insurance required with "adequate cover" | No minimum amount. Ask what cover the landlord expects. |

Fair, ordinary clauses (not flags): 2.2 shared facilities, 4.1 rent due date, 6 utilities, 8.1 subletting by consent, 8.2 quiet hours, 9.3 landlord repairs structure and shared systems, 11.2 termination by the landlord only on statutory grounds, 12 notices and disputes.
